# Siam Bean Co. Mini ERP

One system where sales, stock, invoices and a dashboard share one database, for a small coffee-bean distributor in Bangkok.

## Language

**Company**:
The business that owns the data (the tenant). Every business row carries its `company_id`. Siam Bean Co. is the only company in the scenario; a second one exists only to prove the data stays separate.
_Avoid_: Tenant, Organisation, Account

**User**:
A staff member who logs in. A user belongs to one Company and has exactly one Role.
_Avoid_: Member, Staff, Login

**Role**:
What a User is allowed to do: Admin, Sales, Warehouse or Accountant. Checked on the server; the wrong role gets 403.
_Avoid_: Permission, Group, Level

**Product**:
Something Siam Bean sells, identified by a unique SKU (e.g. ARB-1KG). Has a unit, a cost, a sale price and a Reorder level. Never deleted, only deactivated.
_Avoid_: Item, Article, Good

**SKU**:
The short code that identifies a Product, unique per Company.
_Avoid_: Code, Product ID

**Customer**:
A café that buys from Siam Bean. Has a name, a 13-digit tax ID and Payment terms. Never deleted, only deactivated.
_Avoid_: Client, Buyer, Account

**Payment terms**:
How many days a Customer has to pay an Invoice. 0 days means Cash.
_Avoid_: Credit, Terms

**Stock movement**:
One row that changes stock: positive for goods in, negative for goods out. Every movement references a document (e.g. SO-0001, IN-0001) and the User who made it.
_Avoid_: Transaction, Stock change, Log

**On hand**:
The quantity of a Product in the warehouse, always computed as the sum of its Stock movements. Nobody types it.
_Avoid_: Stock level, Quantity, Inventory

**Stock-in**:
A Stock movement that adds goods received (document IN-000x).
_Avoid_: Goods receipt, Restock

**Adjustment**:
A Stock movement that corrects On hand after a count, damage or loss (document ADJ-000x). Can be positive or negative, but On hand never goes below zero.
_Avoid_: Correction, Write-off

**Reorder level**:
The On hand quantity below which a Product is Low stock.
_Avoid_: Minimum, Threshold, Safety stock

**Low stock**:
A Product whose On hand is below its Reorder level. Shown as a badge and counted on the Dashboard.
_Avoid_: Out of stock, Alert

**Sales order**:
A Customer's request for Products, numbered SO-000x. Starts as Draft, becomes Confirmed (or Cancelled).
_Avoid_: Order, Purchase, Quotation

**Order line**:
One Product on a Sales order with a quantity and the unit price copied from the Product at the time of the order.
_Avoid_: Item, Row, Detail

**Draft**:
A Sales order that can still be edited. It has no effect on stock.
_Avoid_: Pending, New, Quotation

**Confirm**:
The one action that turns a Draft into a Confirmed Sales order. In one transaction it checks stock, creates one negative Stock movement per Order line and issues the Invoice. If any line has too little stock, nothing changes.
_Avoid_: Submit, Approve, Process

**Invoice**:
The bill for a Confirmed Sales order, numbered INV-000x. It stores its own subtotal, VAT and total so that later price changes never touch it. Status is Open or Paid.
_Avoid_: Bill, Receipt, Tax invoice

**VAT**:
Thai value-added tax, 7% of the Invoice subtotal, rounded to the satang.
_Avoid_: Tax

**Payment**:
Money received against one Invoice. When the Payments add up to the Invoice total, the Invoice becomes Paid.
_Avoid_: Receipt, Transfer, Settlement

**Open receivables**:
The total still owed on Open Invoices.
_Avoid_: Outstanding, Debt, AR

**Document number**:
The sequential code of a document, per Company and type (SO-0001, INV-0001, IN-0001, ADJ-0001). Generated by the server, never reused, never edited.
_Avoid_: ID, Reference

**Dashboard**:
The owner's view: Sales today, Low stock count, Open invoices and Stock value, computed live from the same tables.
_Avoid_: Report, Home, Overview

**Sales today**:
The sum of the totals of Invoices issued today (Bangkok time).
_Avoid_: Revenue, Turnover
